# 22 - Review & Readiness

Review for the whole course in two forms: each module's **Q&A review bank** (interview-style questions with model answers, one bank per module) and each module's **quiz** (every *Check Yourself* question from its pages, graded as you go). The [quiz index](/quiz/all) lists all quizzes with question counts.

Start with the **[Readiness Self-Assessment](01-Readiness-Self-Assessment.mdx)**: score yourself 0-3 on the fourteen domains of GenAI engineering, see the target for each, and find the modules, labs and capstones that close your gaps.

```objectives
scope: module
time: As needed - about 45 minutes per module bank
outcomes:
  - Recall and explain the key ideas of any module without the notes open
  - Find weak areas quickly by taking a module quiz, then revisit the linked pages
  - Rehearse interview-style answers with the module Q&A banks
prerequisites:
  - The module you are reviewing
```

## How to Use It

1. Take the [Readiness Self-Assessment](01-Readiness-Self-Assessment.mdx) to pick which modules to review first.
2. Take each module's quiz - it is quick and graded, and each section links back to the page it tests.
3. Revisit the pages where you missed questions, and do their exercises.
4. Use the Q&A bank to practise explaining answers out loud; the model answers show the level of detail interviewers expect.

Earlier versions of the course had separate "Concept Review" pages per topic; their questions were merged into the module banks (duplicates removed, answers checked and updated), and their old links redirect to the banks.
