# 04 - Post-Training & Reasoning

A pretrained base model continues text; it does not follow instructions, refuse harmful requests, or reason step by step on demand. Post-training adds those behaviours: supervised fine-tuning on demonstrations, preference optimization, and reinforcement learning - including the RL-with-verifiable-rewards recipe behind today's reasoning models.

```objectives
scope: module
time: 3-4 hours today (growing as chapters are added)
outcomes:
  - Explain the post-training pipeline from SFT to preference tuning to RL, and what each stage changes in the model
  - Compare RLHF with PPO, DPO-family methods and GRPO by what they need (reward model, critic, online samples) and what they cost
  - Explain how LoRA and QLoRA make fine-tuning affordable, and what they do not fix
  - Describe how RL with verifiable rewards produces long chain-of-thought reasoning
prerequisites:
  - "[LLM Foundations](../01-LLM-Foundations/INDEX.mdx)"
  - "[Pretraining at Scale](../03-Pretraining/INDEX.mdx) - especially the training memory math"
```
